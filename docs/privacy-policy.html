Privacy Policy

Photo Compressor KB & MB · Effective September 28, 2026

Short version: this app has no servers and no accounts. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded, stored by us, or shared. We do not collect any data ourselves. Third parties (Google) handle ads and purchase processing as described below.

1. Information we do not collect

The app works fully offline. It has no backend, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • your photos or any content of your files,
  • your name, email, phone number, or account details (there is no registration),
  • location data — photos are actively stripped of GPS/EXIF location metadata when re-encoded,
  • device identifiers, usage statistics, or diagnostics.

The app uses the internet only to display advertising and to process in-app purchases through Google Play.

2. Information handled by third parties

Google AdMob (advertising)

To keep the app free, we show banner, rewarded, and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google may process information such as your advertising/device identifiers, IP address (which can imply a coarse region), and basic ad interaction data. This is used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information.

Where required (EEA/UK and similar regions), a consent dialog is shown on first launch. You can change or withdraw your ad consent at any time through the “Ad settings / Privacy options” entry the SDK provides. Ads are based on context; we do not combine them with your photos or personal data.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

The optional one-time “lifetime” purchase is sold and processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the app only whether the entitlement is owned on your device, so the purchase can be restored on your account.

3. Your photos

Photos you select are decoded and re-encoded locally. They are placed in the app’s temporary working storage only while a batch runs and are removed automatically afterwards. Finished images are saved to your own gallery (folder Pictures/ImageResizer) and stay fully under your control. Nothing is transmitted to us or anyone else. Data-saver/privacy features (location tags, orientation) follow the documented pipeline: EXIF location is dropped on output.

4. Data retention and deletion

We store no personal data — there is nothing for us to delete. Removing the app from your device removes all of its local data. Purchase records are retained by Google Play under your account’s terms. Ad data is retained by Google under Google’s retention policies.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app’s behavior ever changes (e.g., a new ad provider), this policy will be updated and the revised version will be published at this same URL.
